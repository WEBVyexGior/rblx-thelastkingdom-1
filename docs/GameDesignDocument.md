# 1. Game Vision

## 1.1 Core Identity

**The Last Kingdom** is a medieval co-op story adventure set during the aftermath of a devastating zombie apocalypse.

The game is designed around one central idea:

> A group of forgotten warriors returns home after 40 years away, only to discover that their kingdom has fallen and the people they fought to protect are gone.

The player is not entering an endless survival arena.

The player is returning to a lost world.

The journey is about:

* discovering what happened to the kingdom,
* uncovering forgotten history,
* surviving dangerous encounters,
* restoring what was lost,
* and confronting the source of the corruption.

The game should make players feel:

* curiosity when exploring,
* tension when danger approaches,
* emotional connection with NPCs,
* satisfaction when restoring the kingdom.

---

# 1.2 Design Pillars

## Pillar 1 — Atmosphere Above Everything

Priority: **EXTREMELY HIGH**

The world must feel alive, abandoned and mysterious.

Important elements:

* medieval architecture,
* destroyed villages,
* abandoned castles,
* fog,
* weather,
* environmental storytelling,
* realistic lighting,
* immersive audio.

The player should feel like they entered a forgotten world.

---

## Pillar 2 — Story First

Priority: **EXTREMELY HIGH**

The story is not background information.

The story is the reason the player continues.

The player must constantly discover:

* what happened 40 years ago,
* why the kingdom fell,
* what caused the undead outbreak,
* who survived,
* what is hidden beneath the castle.

Story delivery methods:

* NPC conversations,
* ancient documents,
* letters,
* scrolls,
* environmental clues,
* cinematic moments,
* discoveries.

---

## Pillar 3 — Exploration

Priority: **VERY HIGH**

The world should reward curiosity.

Players should discover:

* hidden locations,
* forgotten camps,
* abandoned houses,
* secret rooms,
* rare items,
* lore collectibles.

Exploration should never feel like empty walking.

---

## Pillar 4 — Combat

Priority: **HIGH**

Combat must feel meaningful.

Combat is not the entire game.

Enemies should create:

* danger,
* tension,
* memorable moments.

The player should remember important battles, not thousands of identical fights.

---

## Pillar 5 — Co-op Experience

Priority: **HIGH**

The game supports up to 8 players.

Playing with friends should improve the experience through:

* teamwork,
* reviving,
* protecting NPCs,
* solving objectives together,
* exploring together.

---

# 2. What The Last Kingdom Is NOT

The Last Kingdom is NOT:

* a traditional wave survival game,
* a Tower Defense style game,
* an endless zombie simulator,
* a game focused only on grinding,
* a pay-to-win experience,
* a short 30-minute Roblox experience.

The goal is to create a memorable adventure that players want to complete and recommend.

---

# 3. Story Overview

## 3.1 The Forgotten Return

Forty years ago, the Kingdom of **Asterfall** was one of the strongest kingdoms in the region.

A mysterious threat appeared beyond the northern borders.

The king sent his best warriors on a long expedition to protect the kingdom.

The mission was expected to last months.

Instead, the warriors disappeared.

Years passed.

The world changed.

When the survivors finally return, they discover:

* their kingdom has fallen,
* their homes are destroyed,
* their families are missing,
* the dead walk again.

The warriors believed they were returning home.

Instead, they discovered they had returned to a graveyard.

---

# 3.2 Main Objective

The player's goal is:

## Restore Asterfall and discover the source of the undead curse.

The journey includes:

1. Reaching the lost kingdom.
2. Discovering what happened during the 40 years.
3. Helping surviving villagers.
4. Recovering lost knowledge.
5. Entering the fallen castle.
6. Defeating the source of the corruption.

---

# 3.3 The Fallen King

The final enemy is not a random monster.

The final boss is:

## The Fallen King of Asterfall

The king attempted to protect his people using an ancient power hidden beneath the castle.

The power was never meant for humans.

Instead of saving the kingdom, it corrupted it.

The king became the source of the undead curse.

The final battle is not only about defeating an enemy.

It is about freeing the last ruler of Asterfall from the corruption that consumed him.

# 4. Campaign Structure

The first game is designed as a complete adventure.

Target completion time:

## Main Story:

2.5 - 3 hours

## Full Completion:

5+ hours

The player should finish the main story feeling that they completed a journey, not just a match.

---

## Chapter 1 — The Return Home

Purpose:
Introduce the world and mystery.

Gameplay:

* arrival at forgotten lands,
* first exploration,
* meeting survivors,
* discovering that Asterfall has fallen.

---

## Chapter 2 — The Lost Villages

Purpose:
Reveal what happened.

Gameplay:

* rescue survivors,
* explore abandoned villages,
* recover documents,
* defend locations.

---

## Chapter 3 — The Road to Asterfall

Purpose:
Increase danger.

Gameplay:

* stronger enemies,
* larger events,
* hidden areas,
* difficult choices.

---

## Chapter 4 — The Fallen Kingdom

Purpose:
Final preparation.

Gameplay:

* enter the castle,
* discover the truth,
* fight through corrupted areas.

---

## Chapter 5 — The Fallen King

Purpose:
Final confrontation.

Gameplay:

* boss battle,
* ending cinematic,
* kingdom restoration.

# 5. Development Priority System

Every feature receives a priority.

## Tier 1 — Critical

Must exist for the game to work.

Examples:

* player movement,
* combat,
* enemy AI,
* mission system,
* objectives,
* map progression,
* saving system.

---

## Tier 2 — Important

Adds quality and depth.

Examples:

* NPC dialogue,
* quests,
* inventory improvements,
* crafting,
* additional enemies,
* events.

---

## Tier 3 — Polish

Implemented after the game works.

Examples:

* advanced animations,
* cinematic effects,
* extra UI effects,
* visual details,
* additional cosmetics.

---

# 6. Waves and Zombie System Philosophy

Zombie waves are NOT the main game loop.

They are story moments.

The game does not use:

Wave 1 → Wave 2 → Wave 3 → Wave 100

as the primary structure.

Instead:

## Story Events

Examples:

* defending a village during an invasion,
* protecting an NPC while opening a gate,
* surviving until reinforcements arrive,
* escaping a collapsing area,
* defending the castle walls.

Each event should feel unique.

For example the player should remember:

"The village was attacked while we were there."

Not:

"We completed wave 34."
